---
title: PDFs
description: Read, annotate, fill in, sign and rearrange PDFs in Zenith's built-in PDF editor.
---

Zenith opens PDFs in its own viewer, where you can also edit them: write on the pages, fill in forms, sign, and rearrange or combine pages.

## Open a PDF

A PDF opens in the viewer when you:

- open a link to a PDF;
- open a PDF file from your computer, for example from File Explorer when Zenith is your app for `.pdf` files;
- type a PDF's address or path in the address bar.

A PDF from the web is downloaded first, with the same sign-in the page had, and then shown. The address bar still shows where the PDF came from.

## Read

- Use the page box to jump to a page, or scroll.
- **Zoom in** and **Zoom out** change the size. Click the zoom percentage to fit the page to the width of the tab.
- **Pages** (the button at the far left) shows a thumbnail of every page; click one to go to it.
- Links in the PDF open in a new tab.

## Annotate

Pick a tool in the toolbar:

- **Text** adds a text box where you click.
- **Draw** draws freehand with the mouse or a pen.
- **Highlight** highlights the text you select.
- **Select** goes back to selecting text and moving or deleting what you added.

The colour square sets the colour for new text, drawings and highlights. **Undo** takes back the last change.

Annotations are saved as standard PDF annotations, so other PDF apps show them and let you edit them.

## Fill in forms

Click a form field and type, or tick checkboxes and pick options. Saving keeps what you entered.

## Sign

1. Choose **Sign**.
2. **Draw** your signature, or **Type** your name. Pick the ink colour if you like.
3. Choose **Place**, then click where the signature goes on the page.

The signature is drawn onto the page as an image. It is not a digital (certificate) signature. Press `Esc` before you click the page to cancel.

## Rearrange pages

Open **Pages**. Under each thumbnail you can rotate the page left or right, move it up or down, insert a blank page after it, or delete it. **Add pages from a PDF** adds every page of another PDF at the end.

## Save

- **Save** (`Ctrl+S`) writes your changes into the file. It is there for PDFs opened from your computer.
- **Save as** (`Ctrl+Shift+S`) asks where to save a copy. Use it for PDFs from the web.

## Good to know

- PDFs that need a password ask for it when they open.
- Scripts inside PDFs don't run.
- Text that is already in the PDF can't be changed; add a text box over it instead.
- A PDF inside part of a page (an embedded viewer) still opens in Chromium's built-in viewer. Open it in its own tab to edit it.
- After a restart, a PDF from the web is downloaded again; a PDF from your computer has to be opened again from its folder.
