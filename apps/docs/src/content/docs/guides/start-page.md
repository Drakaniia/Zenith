The start page is what you see in a new tab: a search box, your saved sites (the **Tableau**) and your most visited sites, over a background image. It makes no network requests of its own.

To use a website as your home page instead, set **Settings › General › Home page**. The home page opens in a window with no tabs and when you press Enter in an empty new tab.

## Saved and most visited sites

- **Most visited** sites come from your local history only. Turn them off with **Settings › General › Show most visited sites on new tabs**.
- Point at a most visited site and choose **Save to Tableau** to keep it, or **Don't show this site** to hide it.
- Saved sites have **Edit** and **Remove** buttons. You can save up to 24 sites and drag tiles to reorder them.

## Customise

Click the **Customize start page** button on the start page. The panel has two tabs.

**Layout**

- **Main page display**: show or hide the **Greeting**, **Search bar**, **Tableau** and **Keyboard hints**, and set the **Position** of the content (Center or Top). **Clear on hover** hides everything while you point at the settings button for a second, so you can see the background; move away to bring it back.
- **Search bar**: its **Width** (Compact or Wide) and the search engine it uses (**Search with**). This is the same search engine setting as in **Settings › General**.
- **Tableau · saved websites**: your saved sites, and **Hidden sites** you removed from most visited, which you can bring back.
- **Tile size**: Small, Medium or Large.

**Background**

- **Gallery**: built-in backgrounds. Use the arrows to preview them.
- **Your images**: add up to 12 of your own pictures.
- **New background every day** moves through the gallery and your images on the first new tab of each day.

Start-page settings and backgrounds belong to the [profile](../profiles/) you're using.
