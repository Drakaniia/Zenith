Glance opens a link in a floating card over the page you're on, so you can read it and come back without managing another tab.

## Open a link in Glance

- `Alt`+click a link on a page.
- Right-click a bookmark and choose **Open in Glance**, or `Alt`+click a bookmark.

The card covers most of the window. The page behind it stays where it was.

## Buttons

Next to the card are round buttons:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Close** | Closes the card. `Esc` or a click outside the card does the same. |
| **Open as tab** | Turns the preview into a normal tab. |
| **Open in split view** | Opens the preview next to the page you opened it from, in [split view](/guides/split-view/). |
| **Copy link** | Copies the preview's address. |

A tab in Glance never goes to sleep while the card is open.

## Extension popups

When you open an extension from the extensions button, its popup appears in a small Glance card next to the button. See [Extensions](/guides/extensions/).

## Other link clicks

- `Ctrl`+click or middle-click a link to open it in a background tab.
- `Shift`+click a bookmark to open it in a new tab.
