---
title: Notes
description: Write quick notes inside Zenith and open them from any app with a keyboard shortcut.
---

Zenith has a simple notes editor for things you want to jot down while browsing.

## Open your notes

- Press `Ctrl+Alt+N`.
- Choose **Notes** in the app menu, or **New note** in the **+** menu at the bottom of the sidebar.
- Use **Open notes** in **Settings › Notes**.

By default the shortcut works even while another app is in front: it brings Zenith forward with your notes. Turn that off with **Settings › Notes › Use the shortcut in every app**. While Zenith is running with this on, other apps don't receive that key combination. If another app already owns it, Zenith tells you and the shortcut only works inside Zenith; pick a different one in **Settings › Keyboard shortcuts**.

## Write and organise

- **New note** starts a note. Give it a title and start writing; notes save as you type.
- The list of notes is grouped by date. Use **Search notes** to filter it.
- Undo and redo with `Ctrl+Z` and `Ctrl+Y`, or the buttons above the note.
- Right-click a note in the list to **Rename** it (`F2`), **Copy text**, or **Delete** it (`Del`). After deleting, **Undo** in the message that appears brings it back.

## Format text

Right-click in a note to see the formatting options and their keys:

- **Bold** `Ctrl+B` and **Italic** `Ctrl+I`. Select text first, or turn the format on and keep typing.
- **Bulleted list** `Ctrl+Shift+8` and **Numbered list** `Ctrl+Shift+7`. Choosing it again turns the list back into plain lines.
- **Cut**, **Copy**, **Paste** and **Select all**.

You can also start a list by typing `- ` or `1. ` at the beginning of a line. While you type in a note, `Ctrl+B` and `Ctrl+I` format text instead of running their usual shortcuts. Text you paste comes in without its formatting. For spelling suggestions, hold `Shift` while you right-click.

Notes are saved as plain text with simple Markdown marks (`**bold**`, `*italic*`, `- ` and `1. ` for lists), so **Copy text** gives you clean text with bullets and numbers.

On wide windows the list of notes is shown next to the editor.

Notes belong to the [profile](../profiles/) you're using.
