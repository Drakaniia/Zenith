---
title: Site safety
description: How Zenith warns about dangerous sites, certificate errors, risky downloads, open Wi-Fi and insecure forms.
---

Zenith checks pages and downloads on your device. Browsed addresses never leave it.

- **Dangerous sites.** Local Safe Browsing compares every address with offline lists of malware and phishing sites and shows a warning page on a match. **Settings › Privacy & blocking › Update lists** refreshes the lists.
- **Certificate errors.** A site whose certificate can't be trusted is blocked until you choose to continue.
- **Risky downloads.** Programs and scripts ask before they are saved, and saved files are marked as downloaded from the internet, so Windows checks them before they run.

## Protect warnings

**Settings › Privacy & blocking › Protect warnings** (on by default) adds two warnings. Neither blocks anything.

- **Open Wi-Fi.** When your computer is connected to a Wi-Fi network without a password (no encryption), Zenith says so once per network each time it starts. Others nearby can see traffic that isn't encrypted, so prefer HTTPS sites there. Zenith checks the connection every minute; without a Wi-Fi adapter it does nothing.
- **Insecure forms.** When you click a password or card number field on a page without HTTPS, Zenith shows *Not secure: don't enter passwords or card numbers here*. Pages served by your own computer (`localhost`) don't warn.
