In compact mode the page fills the window. The sidebar and toolbar are hidden and float in over the page when you need them.

## Turn it on or off

- Press `Ctrl+Alt+C`.
- Choose **Compact mode** in the app menu, or right-click an empty part of the sidebar.
- Use **Settings › Appearance › Compact mode**.
- Drag the sidebar's edge until it's very narrow; Zenith switches to compact mode instead of shrinking the sidebar further.

## Reveal the sidebar and toolbar

Move the pointer to the edge of the window where the sidebar lives. The sidebar slides in over the page and stays while the pointer is over it. Move away and it slides back out.

You can also use the keyboard:

| Shortcut | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Ctrl+Alt+S` | Show or hide the floating sidebar |
| `Ctrl+Alt+T` | Show or hide the floating toolbar |

Opening the address bar (`Ctrl+L`) or the command palette works as usual: they float over the page in every layout.

## With other layouts

Compact mode works with every **Sidebar position** and **Layout** in **Settings › Appearance**. In the single-toolbar layout, the window controls (minimise, maximise, close) also appear when the pointer is at the top edge.

Because the floating sidebar draws over the page instead of pushing it aside, pages keep rendering underneath, and videos keep playing.
