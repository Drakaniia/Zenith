## What you get

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Vertical tabs and spaces" icon="list-format">
		Tabs live in a sidebar. Group them into spaces, each with its own name, emoji and accent colour.
		Essentials and pinned tabs stay one click away.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Split view and Glance" icon="seti:layout">
		Put two tabs side by side with Ctrl+Alt+S, or Alt+click a link to preview it in a floating card
		without leaving the page.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Compact mode" icon="laptop">
		Press Ctrl+Alt+C and the page fills the window. The sidebar and toolbar float in from the edges
		when you need them.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Ad and tracker blocking" icon="approve-check">
		EasyList and EasyPrivacy are built in, with custom lists, a per-site toggle and a counter of
		blocked requests.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Passwords and payment cards" icon="seti:lock">
		Save sign-ins and cards encrypted on your PC, and fill them in on login and checkout forms.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Small and quick" icon="rocket">
		A 1.78 MB installer, a visible window in under a second, and about 93 MB of app overhead with one
		tab open. No telemetry and no accounts.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
